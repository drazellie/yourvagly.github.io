About me

I turn messy inboxes, packed calendars and scattered files into simple systems you can rely on.

I'm Glyzel, a virtual and executive assistant from Pasay, Philippines, with a degree in information technology and more than five years of hands-on work behind the scenes of real businesses.

I've been the right hand of executives at a trucking and construction company, run HR, inventory and vendors for a retail store, grown social media pages for client brands, kept medical and insurance records accurate for a laboratory, solved customer issues in tech support, organized events and tutored students.

Business owners usually come to me when they're doing everything themselves: replying to emails at midnight, missing follow-ups, posting whenever they remember, and keeping records in five different places. I take that work off your plate, set up simple systems that keep it running, and send you clear updates so you always know what's done.
